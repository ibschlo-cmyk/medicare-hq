## Authority order used by the project

1. **Current controlled Aetna production workflow** — system navigation, permissions, routing, required documentation.
2. **Exact 2026 EOC / Member Handbook** — plan benefits, rights, coverage rules, billing, appeals and plan-specific constraints.
3. **Current Aetna plan/provider/member pages and 2026 forms** — current contacts, vendor/provider support, PA forms, benefit guides.
4. **Recent controlled training material** — operational structure subject to current-production confirmation.
5. **Trainer notes** — leads to verify, not proof of universality.
6. **CMS/state authority** — regulatory validation and context.
7. **Generic foundation lessons** — background only.

## Dated-source red flags already identified

### Benefits Information script — 02/23/2025

Useful:

- current-plan Member Handbook/EOC first;
- plan-specific benefit quote;
- check PA requirements rather than assume;
- CM/SC coordination when appropriate;
- required closing/documentation behavior.

Do **not** carry forward without reconciliation:

- legacy MMP/Ohio plan list;
- generic 365-day OON/transition language;
- blanket urgent-care network phrasing;
- generalized D-SNP contact hours/time zone;
- obsolete state/product-specific examples.

### PHI-copy workflow — through 12/2024

Treat this as a **separate request-for-records process**, not the default caller-verification process. The supplied source differentiates ordinary account authentication from a member/authorized representative requesting copies of claim/PHI records and includes a distinct fulfillment/documentation workflow. Current production guidance must confirm operational details before inclusion in a private live-call aid.

## Final publication rule

No statement enters the quick-reference desk aid solely because it appears in an older training screenshot. Each operational statement must be either:

- supported by current controlled workflow,
- supported by the exact 2026 plan source,
- or explicitly labeled as a trainer/internal item pending confirmation.
