# 2026 Medicare Savings Program (MSP) + Extra Help — Operational Quick Reference

**Updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Primary authority:** Medicare.gov / CMS 2026 limits

## Operational distinction

| Level | Helps pay | High-value Member Services implication |
|---|---|---|
| **QMB** | Part A premium if applicable, Part B premium, **Medicare Part A/B deductibles, coinsurance and copayments** | **Federal QMB balance-billing protection applies** to Medicare-covered Part A/B items/services |
| **SLMB** | Part B premium | Does **not** by itself create the QMB Part A/B cost-sharing billing prohibition |
| **QI** | Part B premium | Does **not** by itself create QMB cost-sharing protection; QI is for people who do not qualify for other Medicaid coverage/benefits |
| **QDWI** | Part A premium | Different population/eligibility rules; no QMB cost-sharing protection from QDWI alone |

QMB, SLMB and QI eligibility also confers **Extra Help (LIS)** for Medicare prescription-drug costs.

## 2026 federal MSP income/resource limits

These are **federal baseline limits**, not a substitute for the member's actual state eligibility record. States may use higher limits or different counting rules.

| MSP | Individual monthly income | Married couple monthly income | Individual resources | Couple resources |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **QMB** | $1,350 | $1,824 | $9,950 | $14,910 |
| **SLMB** | $1,616 | $2,184 | $9,950 | $14,910 |
| **QI** | $1,816 | $2,455 | $9,950 | $14,910 |
| **QDWI** | $5,405 | $7,299 | $4,000 | $6,000 |

Income limits are slightly higher in Alaska/Hawaii. State rules may be more generous.

## 2026 Extra Help / LIS — Rx cost branch

CMS 2026 LIS guidance shows:

- institutionalized / qualifying HCBS full-benefit duals: **$0** deductible and **$0** covered-drug copays;
- full-benefit duals at or below 100% FPL: up to **$1.60 generic / $4.90 brand**;
- many other LIS categories, including QMB-only, SLMB-only and QI: up to **$5.10 generic / $12.65 brand**;
- Part D out-of-pocket threshold for 2026: **$2,100**, after which covered Part D drugs have $0 cost sharing.

Do not quote an LIS copay from the member's MSP acronym alone when the exact LIS category/system status is available.

## Live-call classifiers

### “Why am I getting a bill?”

`Claim -> MSP/QMB status ON DATE OF SERVICE -> Medicare-covered Part A/B cost share? -> QMB protection if applicable -> provider correction/current Aetna workflow`

### “Why is my Part B premium coming out of Social Security?”

`Current MSP eligibility/effective date -> QMB/SLMB/QI status -> state buy-in/update timing -> current eligibility/escalation workflow`

### “My prescription copay is wrong”

`Part D claim -> current LIS category/effective date -> BAE/LIS process if system level is wrong -> do not treat as ordinary formulary/medical PA issue`

## Critical red-team rules

- **QMB ≠ SLMB ≠ QI.** Only QMB includes the federal Medicare Part A/B cost-sharing billing protection.
- **Current status ≠ status on old date of service.** Historical bill calls require historical eligibility/MSP verification.
- **Federal income limits ≠ state eligibility determination.** The four D-SNP states may apply state-specific rules.
- **MSP level ≠ exact Medicaid benefit package.** Full-benefit Medicaid and MSP-only status must remain distinct.
