# QMB Billing Protection — 2026 Live-Call Branch

**Updated:** 2026-08-08  
**Primary authority:** CMS Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) Program / MLN billing guidance

## Why this belongs in the “I got a bill” workflow

CMS states that federal law prohibits Medicare providers and suppliers — including Medicare Advantage providers and suppliers — from billing a member in the **Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB)** group for Medicare Part A or Part B cost sharing on Medicare-covered items and services.

QMBs have **no legal obligation** to pay Medicare Part A/B deductibles, coinsurance or copayments for Medicare-covered items/services.

The prohibition applies even when:

- the provider does not participate in Medicaid;
- Medicaid pays less than the full Medicare cost sharing;
- Medicaid pays nothing;
- the QMB benefit is from a different state than the provider.

## High-speed bill branch

`Member got bill -> identify service/date -> locate claim -> verify QMB status ON DATE OF SERVICE -> verify Medicare-covered service + claim disposition -> determine whether amount is prohibited Medicare cost sharing -> provider correction / current plan escalation -> document`

## Critical rules

### 1. QMB status is date-of-service specific

CMS says QMB billing protections apply to dates of service on which the beneficiary was a QMB.

For a historical bill, do not rely only on today's eligibility status.

### 2. Provider cannot make the QMB pay because Medicaid did not pay enough

State Medicaid programs may limit what they pay toward Medicare cost sharing. That does not shift prohibited Medicare Part A/B cost sharing to the QMB member.

### 3. Provider type does not erase the QMB protection

CMS guidance applies to Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage providers/suppliers, not just providers that accept Medicaid.

### 4. If QMB member already paid prohibited cost sharing

CMS instructs providers/suppliers to remedy improper QMB billing and collections. The live-call workflow should identify the claim, amount and date of service and use the current Aetna/provider correction or reimbursement/escalation process rather than telling the member the payment was necessarily valid.

## What QMB does NOT prove by itself

QMB protection is specific to **Medicare Part A and Part B cost sharing for Medicare-covered items and services**.

Before applying the QMB rule, verify:

- exact service/date;
- whether the service is Medicare covered;
- whether the amount billed is Medicare deductible/coinsurance/copayment rather than a noncovered item or other charge;
- claim/network/authorization context;
- member's QMB status for that date of service.

## Desk-aid trigger

**QMB ON DOS?**

`YES -> Medicare-covered Part A/B service? -> billed amount is Medicare cost share? -> member should not be billed that cost share -> provider correction/current Aetna workflow`

`NO / UNKNOWN -> continue ordinary bill/claim/member-responsibility workflow`
