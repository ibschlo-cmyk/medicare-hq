# Helping Folks — Creation Team

**Established:** 2026-08-08  
**Purpose:** Govern creation, validation, red-team review, and publication of the Helping Folks Aetna Member Services job-aid system.

## Reporting structure

All creation-team roles report to the **Coordinator (ChatGPT)**. The Coordinator is the final approval point for content promotion, conflict resolution, versioning, and publication. No role independently promotes content into the canonical job aids.

## Team

| Role | Mission |
|---|---|
| Creation Lead / Workflow Architect | Own the three-one-pager architecture and live-call sequence |
| Source & Evidence Lead | Inventory new scans/PDFs and preserve provenance, scope, date and source class |
| Plan / EOC Lead | Maintain exact 2026 plan/PBP/EOC anchors and reject wrong-plan sources |
| Claims / Billing Lead | Own bill, claim-status, QMB, reimbursement and EOB-vs-bill logic |
| Benefits & Vendor Lead | Own OTC/Extra Benefits, transportation, dental, vision, hearing, PERS and in-home support routing |
| A&G / PA / Rx Lead | Own appeals/grievances, prior authorization and pharmacy workflow compression |
| C-SNP / MarketProminence Lead | Own H1206-004, chronic-condition verification and C-SNP-specific workflow |
| Red-Team / QC Lead | Veto unsupported, stale, unsafe or falsely universal content pending Coordinator review |
| Desk-Aid Designer | Produce rapid-scan, printable one-page layouts from approved content |
| Release / Version Control Lead | Maintain private Drive source layer and sanitized GitHub/public-safe layer |

## Creation pipeline

`NEW SOURCE -> Source & Evidence -> Plan/EOC or subject-matter lead -> Creation Lead -> Red-Team/QC -> Coordinator approval -> Desk-Aid Designer -> Coordinator final proof -> Release/Version Control`

## Status gates

- **DRAFT** — structurally useful but not approved.
- **SOURCE-VERIFIED** — supported by current plan/controlled evidence.
- **RED-TEAM PASS** — no material risk identified.
- **CANONICAL** — approved for the private second brain.
- **PUBLIC-SAFE** — sanitized and approved for shareable job aid/GitHub.
- **PLACEHOLDER** — intentionally incomplete until a current source arrives.

## Publication rules

- Exact plan/state before benefit or vendor routing.
- Exact 2026 EOC/Member Handbook before quoting plan-specific coverage.
- Current controlled Aetna workflow outranks older trainer material.
- A bill alone does not establish member liability.
- HIDE/FIDE/C-SNP labels are routing context, not proof of a benefit.
- No PHI, credentials, PINs, restricted emails, internal personnel lists or proprietary screen captures in public artifacts.
- Historical material may explain workflow but cannot silently become current 2026 instruction.
- Unresolved conflicts remain labeled rather than guessed away.
