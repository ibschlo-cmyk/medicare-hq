## Content controls

Every final statement was reviewed for:

1. authoritative source support;
2. currency as of July 24, 2026;
3. correct classification as federal, Aetna, state-specific, plan-specific, or internal workflow information;
4. benefit-promising risk;
5. false assumptions that one entity administers every Medicaid service; and
6. usability during a live member call.

The job aids direct representatives to verify the member's exact plan, state, eligibility, Evidence of Coverage, Summary of Benefits, member handbook, and current controlled internal workflow before quoting benefits or routing services.

## Principal source hierarchy

1. Current eCFR and federal regulations
2. CMS Medicare Managed Care Manual and 2026 enrollment guidance
3. CMS D-SNP integration materials and datasets
4. Current Aetna Medicare public materials
5. Aetna Better Health and applicable state Medicaid materials
6. Photographed Aetna training and operational materials, preserved as source evidence but revalidated before use

## Known publication limitation

The uploaded Aetna training materials included older revision dates and at least one obsolete 2026 plan reference. The source-validation memorandum identifies those issues. These PDFs should remain a peer-created reference until reviewed under the employer's current document-control and approval process.
